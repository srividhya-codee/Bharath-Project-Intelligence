DOCUMENT TYPE: Meeting Minutes
DATE: September 10, 2024

PARTICIPANTS: District Revenue Officer, Project Director NHAI, Lead Contractor Rep
PAGE: 2 of 3

RECORD OF DISCUSSION & DECISIONS:

Item 1: Land Acquisition and Compensation Disbursement
The District Administration reported that out of Rs. 195.00 Cr allocated for land compensation, Rs. 162.50 Cr has been deposited with the Competent Authority for Land Acquisition (CALA). However, disbursement in 3 revenue villages is stalled due to title disputes in the Sub-Court.
DECISION: District Revenue Officer to set up a dedicated Lok Adalat fast-track camp within 14 days to resolve remaining title claims.

Item 2: Financial-Physical Disparity and Contractor Mobilization
The Chair noted that financial disbursement (84.0%) significantly exceeds physical completion (62.0%). The Contractor clarified that Rs. 140.00 Cr was disbursed as mobilization and machinery advance, alongside advance procurement of bitumen and structural steel.
DECISION: Independent Engineer to conduct milestone-based physical verification before clearing subsequent IPC (Interim Payment Certificate) releases.

Item 3: Schedule Recovery Plan
Contractor committed to augmenting machinery deployed: additional 2 asphalt paving trains and 18 heavy dumpers by October 1, 2024, to recover at least 6% of the 18% physical progress gap prior to peak Northeast monsoon.
