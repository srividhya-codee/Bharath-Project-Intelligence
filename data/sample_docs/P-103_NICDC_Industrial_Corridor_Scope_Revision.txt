1. REVISED COST ESTIMATE (RCE) APPRAISAL
- Original Approved Cost: Rs. 2,100.00 Cr
- Revised Estimated Cost (RCE-I): Rs. 2,680.00 Cr
- Cost Overrun: +Rs. 580.00 Cr (+27.6% variance)
- Planned Physical Progress: 55.0% | Actual Physical Progress: 51.0% (SPI: 0.927)

2. ROOT CAUSES OF COST VARIANCE:
(a) Major Scope Expansion: Post-award addition of a 45 MLD Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD) common effluent treatment plant and an automated 12-track railway freight interchange siding not envisaged in the preliminary feasibility study.
(b) Inflationary Adjustment on Structural Steel & Cement: Base index escalation under standard price revision clauses accounted for Rs. 142.00 Cr.
(c) Elevated Multi-Level Grade Separators: Redesign to accommodate NHAI expressway crossing clearances required redesigning 3 elevated flyovers.
Status: Revised Cost Estimate has been submitted to the Public Investment Board (PIB) for formal sanction.
