2. AUDIT FINDINGS:
(a) Massive Advance Disbursement for HDPE / DI Pipe Procurement: Out of Rs. 512 Cr expenditure, Rs. 210 Cr was disbursed to pipe manufacturers without delivery at village distribution centers.
(b) Stalled Village Reticulation Networks: In-village household tap connection piping is stalled across 124 gram panchayats due to pending road-cutting permissions from Rural Works Department.
(c) High Risk Flag: Early warning engine has classified P-105 as Critical Risk (Risk Score 86/100) due to funds utilisation significantly outpacing commissioned physical infrastructure.
