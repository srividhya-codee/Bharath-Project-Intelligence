MINISTRY: Ministry of Finance, Department of Expenditure
DOCUMENT TYPE: Circular / Financial Guidelines
SUBJECT: Appraisal and Approval Procedure for Revised Cost Estimates (RCE) in Central Infrastructure Projects Exceeding Rs. 500 Crore
PAGE: 1 of 2

1. REVISED COST THRESHOLDS
In accordance with Cabinet Secretariat instructions, any infrastructure project under central ministries experiencing:
(a) Cost overrun exceeding 10% of the originally approved capital expenditure, OR
(b) Time overrun exceeding 12 months from original sanction date,
shall mandatorily undergo appraisal by the Committee of Secretaries followed by approval by the Public Investment Board (PIB) / Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA).

2. DETERMINATION OF ACCOUNTABILITY
Every RCE submission must classify variance drivers into two categories:
- Controllable Factors: Delays in tender finalization, inadequate contractor supervision, contractual laxity, or unapproved scope modifications.
- Uncontrollable Factors: Statutory environmental court stays, natural disasters, geologically unforeseen tunneling complications, or wholesale price index escalation.
Officers must incorporate Earned Value metrics (SPI, CPI, EAC) in all revised appraisal memorandums.
