2. CORE BOTTLENECK: POWER EVACUATION SYSTEM MISMATCH
Generation capacity (400 MW DC installed) is ready for generation. However, power evacuation is physically blocked because the 400/220 kV Inter-State Transmission System (ISTS) pooling sub-station being constructed by the designated transmission utility has suffered a 7-month delay. The transmission delay was triggered by Right-of-Way disputes over 18 transmission tower footings across private agricultural farmland.
Recommendation: Coordinate urgent tripartite meeting between MNRE, Central Electricity Authority (CEA), and CTUIL to fast-track tower stringing.
