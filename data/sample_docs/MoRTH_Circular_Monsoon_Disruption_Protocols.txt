MINISTRY: Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH)
DOCUMENT TYPE: Circular
SUBJECT: Standard Operating Protocol for Time Extension (EOT) on Account of Abnormal Monsoon Disruption and Force Majeure
PAGE: 1 of 2

1. BACKGROUND
Severe localized precipitation events and extended monsoon seasons frequently impede bituminous laying and earthwork operations across national highway packages. Disparate regional interpretations have led to contractual disputes and litigation.

2. POLICY DIRECTIVES:
(a) Verification of Rain Gauge Records: Contractor claims for weather delay must be substantiated with official Indian Meteorological Department (IMD) rainfall observatory data showing rainfall exceeding 150% of the long-term regional average.
(b) Non-Levy of Liquidated Damages: Where rainfall prevents execution of critical path activities (GSB, WMM, Bituminous surfacing), Project Directors are authorized to grant interim schedule relief without imposing liquidated damages, provided non-monsoon milestones are maintained.
(c) Idle Machinery Compensation: No idle equipment charges shall be admissible unless explicit standing orders to demobilize were withheld by the Authority's Engineer.
