1. EXECUTIVE BRIEF
The Detailed Project Report (DPR) outlines the capacity augmentation of the existing two-lane carriageway to a four-lane dual carriageway configuration spanning 78.4 km in Tamil Nadu. The project objective is to de-congest the regional freight corridor, improve logistics velocity, and enhance road safety standards.

2. FINANCIAL APPRAISAL & BUDGET ALLOCATION
- Total Approved Capital Cost: Rs. 1,250.00 Crore (Civil Works: Rs. 980.00 Cr; Land Acquisition & R&R: Rs. 195.00 Cr; Utility Shifting & Environmental Mitigation: Rs. 75.00 Cr).
- Concession / EPC Model: Engineering, Procurement, Construction (EPC) standard mode.
- Initial Scheduled Date of Completion: 36 calendar months from Appointed Date (March 31, 2025).

3. KEY RIGHT-OF-WAY (RoW) REQUIREMENTS
Total land required for 60-meter RoW across 14 revenue villages is 164.8 hectares, comprising:
- Private agricultural land: 112.4 hectares (Section 3A/3D notifications issued).
- Government revenue poramboke land: 38.2 hectares.
- Reserved forest fringe diversion: 14.2 hectares requiring Stage-I FC clearance under Forest Conservation Act.

4. ANTICIPATED CRITICAL PATH RISKS
The preliminary alignment survey identifies three primary bottlenecks:
(a) Acquisition of private land pockets in District revenue taluks.
(b) Heavy underground utility relocation (TANGEDCO electrical lines and TWAD drinking water pipes).
(c) Vulnerability to intense Northeast monsoon rainfall between October and December.
