STATUS OF STATUTORY CLEARANCES:
1. Stage-I Approval: Granted on November 14, 2022, subject to 12 stipulated conditions, including transfer of non-forest land for Compensatory Afforestation (CA) and payment of Net Present Value (NPV).
2. Compliance Status: Payment of NPV amounting to Rs. 14.82 Cr was credited into the CAMPA fund in February 2023. Mutation of CA land (28.4 hectares in Taluk revenue jurisdiction) was completed in April 2024.
3. Formal Stage-II Order: Formal working permission under Section 2 of the Forest Conservation Act has been issued. Tree felling permission for 4,210 enumerated trees along the median and flanks is 80% executed. No statutory environmental impediment remains on the forest section.
